Our approach

Holistic, integrative, and built for a win where everyone lives.

Families seldom come to me because they have stopped loving one another. They come because they have stopped being able to hear one another, and neither side can say quite when that happened.

Tulip works across mental, emotional and relational health rather than treating any one of them alone. Where there is conflict, mediation offers a collaborative route: parties identify the solution together, all voices are heard, and nobody has to lose for the family to move.

Every plan is tailor made. Neutrality, reframing and interest based negotiation serve a family in difficulty as faithfully as they serve parties before a court.

Programmes

Roots and Wings

Growing secure, confident, connected families.

Roots and Wings is Tulip’s family formation programme. Each edition takes one relationship in the family and works it from both sides at once: a set of sessions for each person, and a bridge exercise where the two of you meet and read your answers aloud.

Roots means secure attachment and belonging. Wings means healthy autonomy, identity and responsibility. The programme exists because no family should have to trade one for the other.

  • 01 Adolescents and their parents Ages 11 to 17 · ten paired sessions · workbook and reflection journal Available
  • 02 Single parents Raising alone, without carrying it alone In development
  • 03 Blended families Two histories, one household In development

The therapist

Patricia Michubu

Founder and Lead Therapist, Tulip Family Therapy, Nairobi

She holds a Master of Arts in Marriage and Family Therapy, a Diploma in Transformational Church Leadership, and a Certificate in Child Safeguarding and Protection. Her clinical training includes international training at Trinity Western University, Langley, British Columbia. She is reading for a PhD in Marriage and Family Therapy at Pan Africa Christian University.

She is a Licensed Marriage and Family Therapist, registered with the Counsellors and Psychologists Board, and an accredited Mediator with the Judiciary of Kenya. Family therapy and Alternative Dispute Resolution meet daily in her consulting room. Her practice is grounded in a Christian understanding of the person, held alongside the clinical evidence rather than in place of it.
