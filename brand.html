Internal reference

Two devices. Never both on one page.

This page exists so the identity survives the next programme, and the one after that. Everything below is already in use. If a new page is built, it should take one of the two devices and nothing else.

The signatures

The ripple, and the horizon.

Tulip has two visual devices and they carry different meanings. Keeping them apart is what stops the practice and its programmes blurring into each other.

Device one

The Ripple

Concentric arcs widening from the right edge. It stands for reach: the work spreading outward from one family to a school, a congregation, an institution. It is quiet, it sits behind the type, and it never crosses a headline.

  • Use on: the practice. Home, services, about, contact
  • Colours: gold, pale gold and slate, on cream
  • Position: centred off the right edge, masked out on the left
  • Opacity: about 0.6 on desktop, 0.4 on mobile

Device two

The Horizon

Cream above, deep teal below, the tulip sitting on the line with its roots growing down into the dark. It stands for roots and wings, belonging and becoming. It also reproduces the printed workbook cover, so book and screen read as one object.

  • Use on: programme pages only. Roots and Wings, and whatever follows it
  • The tulip always sits on the line, never floating above it
  • Roots grow downward once on load, then never move again
  • Keep text clear of the roots by the full root height plus a margin
  • One device per pageNever place the ripple and the horizon on the same page. That was the original mistake and it made the homepage read as a programme page.
  • Programmes get the horizonA future programme sitting beside Roots and Wings inherits the horizon, with its own title and colour emphasis.
  • The practice gets the rippleAnything describing Tulip itself rather than a programme takes the ripple.
  • Neither device carries textBoth sit behind the content at low opacity. If a device fights a headline, move the device, not the headline.

Type

Two families, three jobs.

The brand face is Mazzard H SemiBold, used in the logo. It is not licensed for the web, so Poppins stands in as its closest free relative. If a licence is bought, swap the font files and change nothing else.

Display
Where families learn to hear one another again.
Voice
Growing secure, confident, connected families.
Body
Poppins Light and Regular for reading. Lora Italic is reserved for Patricia’s own words and for programme subtitles. Never set a paragraph in Lora.
Label
Eyebrows are Poppins 600, tracked 0.24em, uppercase, gold

All six font files are self hosted in assets/fonts and total 77 KB. There is no request to Google, which keeps the site fast on a mobile connection and removes a third party dependency.

Adding a new programme

Copy the Roots and Wings page. Keep the horizon. Change everything else.

Each programme gets its own folder under programmes/ with its own index.html. It inherits the horizon, the header, the footer and assets/tulip.css. Full instructions are in GITHUB_GUIDE.html in the same folder as this site.
